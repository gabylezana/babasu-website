# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, co-investors and ecosystem partners evaluating Babasu Ventures, its investment perspective and the companies it actively supports across Latin America.

## Product Purpose

Present Babasu Ventures as a disciplined, operator-led early-stage investor. The site should make its regional focus, investment philosophy, team and current portfolio easy to understand and trust.

## Brand Personality

Calm, grounded and assured. The experience should feel premium without becoming flashy, corporate or distant.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup templates, overly dark interfaces, purple accents, excessive glass effects, heavy overlays, decorative motion and verbose hero copy. Avoid changes that detach internal pages from the existing Babasu identity.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a clear investment point of view.
- Keep photography central and legible.
- Prefer restraint, clarity and cohesion over decorative effects.
- Explain portfolio companies through concrete operating value.
- Preserve a consistent visual language across every public page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, meaningful alternative text, sufficient color contrast and reduced-motion behavior. Target WCAG 2.1 AA for public-facing content.
